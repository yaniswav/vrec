# One video link per line.
#
# Lines starting with "#" are ignored, and so are duplicate links.
# Duplicates ignore tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, ref, ref_src, si, feature), a
# trailing slash and a #fragment. Links that differ in any other query parameter, such as
# ?v=A and ?v=B, are different videos.
#
# Two formats are supported, and can be mixed freely:
#
# 1) A plain link on its own line:
https://example.com/videos/sample-one

# 2) A title line followed by its link on the next line, as produced by
#    "copy all tabs" browser extensions. The title is then used as the
#    recorded file's name.
Sample Video Two
https://example.com/videos/sample-two

Sample Video Three
https://example.com/videos/sample-three
